## Main Notes

## 1. Securitization

**Securitization** converts a pool of loans or receivables into tradable fixed income securities.

The securities are backed by cash flows from the underlying collateral pool.

---

## 2. Basic Process

1. A bank or corporation creates a pool of debt-based assets.
2. The asset pool is sold to a separate legal entity.
3. The separate entity issues asset-backed securities to investors.
4. Cash flows from the collateral are used to pay investors.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Originator** | Entity that creates the loans or receivables |
| **Seller / Depositor** | Entity that sells assets into the structure |
| **SPE** | Special purpose entity that owns the collateral |
| **Servicer** | Entity that collects payments from borrowers |
| **ABS investor** | Investor who buys securities backed by collateral cash flows |

## 3. Role of the SPE

The SPE separates the collateral from the originator.

This structure can make the collateral **bankruptcy remote**, meaning investors rely primarily on the asset pool rather than the originator's overall credit.

---

## 4. Benefits to Issuers

| Benefit | Explanation |
|------|------|
| Increased business activity | Banks can originate more loans by moving loans off balance sheet |
| Improved profitability | Originator can earn fees from origination and servicing |
| Lower capital reserves | Selling loans can reduce required regulatory capital |
| Improved liquidity | Illiquid loan pools are converted into tradable securities |

---

## 5. Benefit to Investors

Securitization connects capital providers with borrowers in the collateral pool.

Investors can gain exposure to specific asset classes such as:

- auto loans
- credit card receivables
- mortgages
- corporate loans

---

## 6. Key Risks

| Risk | Description |
|------|------|
| Credit risk | Borrowers in the collateral pool may default |
| Prepayment risk | Borrowers may repay earlier than expected |
| Extension risk | Cash flows may arrive later than expected |
| Structural risk | Tranche priority can change risk and return |

### Exam Points

- Securitization removes the originator from the direct intermediation process.
- SPE structure separates collateral cash flows from originator credit.
- The servicer collects payments and passes them through the structure.
- ABS investors bear risk tied to the collateral pool and security structure.
