This site is becoming my public finance notebook.

The focus is intentionally narrow: valuation, market structure, financial modeling,
and real transaction case studies. I want the blog to become a place where each
post does one useful job: clarify a concept, document a model, explain a deal, or
turn a messy question into a reusable framework.

## Why write it in public?

Finance is easy to consume and hard to internalize. Reading a curriculum chapter
or watching a modeling lesson can feel productive, but the real test is whether I
can explain the idea clearly without hiding behind jargon.

Writing in public forces that test.

Every post should make future study easier. A CFA reading note should become a
clean reference. A BIWS exercise should become a modeling checklist. A deal case
study should become a template for thinking through strategic rationale, valuation,
structure, risk, and market reaction.

## The content system

The blog will run on three tracks:

| Track | Cadence | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CFA and BIWS notes | 2-3 posts per week | Convert study material into searchable explanations |
| Deal case studies | 1 post per week | Build judgment by analyzing real transactions |
| Research logs | 1-2 posts per week | Capture questions, observations, and early ideas |

The important part is not just publishing more. It is compounding the archive.
Each note should connect to a formula, a glossary term, a case study, or a future
modeling exercise.

## How I will use AI

AI will help with speed, but not with judgment.

The workflow is:

1. Collect sources, filings, lecture notes, and examples.
2. Ask AI to identify the core concepts, edge cases, and missing questions.
3. Build my own explanation, tables, and examples.
4. Use AI as an editor to challenge unclear claims or weak structure.
5. Publish the note with enough context that I can reuse it later.

The rule is simple: AI can compress the path to understanding, but the final
thinking has to be mine.

## The first milestone

For the next 60 days, I want this site to become a visible record of disciplined
finance study:

- 16+ study notes
- 8+ transaction case studies
- 8+ research logs
- A cleaner glossary and formula library
- A repeatable template for deal analysis

That is enough to make the blog useful, searchable, and honest. The rest can
compound from there.
