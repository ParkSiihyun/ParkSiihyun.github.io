# Bloomberg Terminal Content System

This file is a working map for turning Bloomberg Terminal access into repeatable
blog content.

## Ground Rule

Do not publish proprietary Bloomberg screenshots, exported tables, or raw data
dumps unless the school license and Bloomberg terms clearly allow it.

Use Bloomberg to research faster. Publish original interpretation, clean
self-made tables, and public-source citations.

## Recurring Series

### 1. Deal Teardown

Format: long-form case study.

Example titles:

- Why Microsoft Bought LinkedIn
- Salesforce-Slack: Distribution, Workflow, and Strategic Premium
- Adobe-Figma: When Strategic Logic Meets Regulatory Risk
- Broadcom-VMware: Scale, Leverage, and Infrastructure Software

### 2. Bloomberg Function Diary

Format: short post explaining one Bloomberg function through one real example.

Example titles:

- What `RV <GO>` Taught Me About Relative Valuation
- Using `FA <GO>` to Read a Company Faster
- Why `GP <GO>` Matters After a Deal Announcement
- How `EE <GO>` Changes a Valuation Conversation

### 3. Market Context Before the Deal

Format: research log.

Example titles:

- What Rates Looked Like Before the Deal
- Sector Multiples Before the Announcement
- Why Financing Conditions Matter in M&A

### 4. Filing vs Terminal

Format: verification post.

Example titles:

- What Bloomberg Summarized vs What the Proxy Actually Said
- Why I Still Read the Merger Agreement
- How Deal Terms Change the Headline Story

### 5. IB Interview Prep With Live Companies

Format: concept explainer using a real public company.

Example titles:

- EV vs Equity Value Using Microsoft
- Revenue Synergies vs Cost Synergies Using a Real Deal
- Why Stock Deals Share Risk
- What Deal Spread Means After Announcement

## Weekly Workflow

### Monday

Pick one deal from `MA <GO>` or current news.

Output: short research log.

### Tuesday

Build buyer and target profiles using `DES <GO>` and `FA <GO>`.

Output: company snapshot table.

### Wednesday

Check valuation using `RV <GO>`, `EE <GO>`, `GP <GO>`, and `HP <GO>`.

Output: valuation bridge.

### Thursday

Read public filings and deal documents.

Output: verified deal terms and source notes.

### Friday

Write the case study.

Output: blog post.

### Weekend

Turn the case study into:

- 1 short LinkedIn post
- 1 visual valuation table
- 1 function diary
- 1 research log follow-up
