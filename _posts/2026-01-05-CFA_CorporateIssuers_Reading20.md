## Main Notes

## 1. Organizational Forms

| Form | 필기 정리 |
|------|-----------|
| Sole proprietorship | a business owned and operated by an individual |
| General partnership | two or more individuals, partnership agreement 필요 |
| Limited partnership | one or more general partners operate the business and have unlimited liability |
| LLP(Limited liability partnership) | general partner is not required, all partners are limited partners |

Limited partners는 partnership에 투자한 금액까지만 책임진다.

## 2. Corporation & Limited Company

- 다른 조직과는 다르게 하나의 법적 주체로 인정받는다
- 회사의 주인과 경영인이 다르다
- board of directors : responsible for hiring the senior managers to operate the company
- 주식회사와
- LC(LLC) : limited company 유한책임회사
- Inc(incorporated) : corporation과 거의 유사

## 3. Public vs Private Company

| Type | 필기 정리 |
|------|-----------|
| Public corporation | listed corporation, public limited company, 상장회사 |
| Private limited company | limited number of shareholders and restrictions on transfer of shares |

LC(LLC) 와 Corporation의 차이는 뭐냐?
- LLC 앞에 붙은 limited liability는 corporation의 주주들도 유한책임이기 때문에 큰 차이점이 되지
않음
- 진짜 차이 : LLC는 이사회가 없어도 되고, 수익배분이 조금 더 자유롭고, 그래서 작은 사업이나 가족
회사 느낌이 강함

## 4. Articles of Incorporation

Articles of incorporation : 기업의 정관
- as a legal entity, a corporation has many of the rights and responsibilities of an individual
- Shareholders voting rights : elect the board of directors
- The board of directors distribute a portion of the company’s earnings to the shareholders
as dividends
-> Double taxations : 이익에도 세금이 붙고, 배당에도 세금이 붙는 형태

## 5. Public LC and Private LC

public LC
- most of public LC are listed company
Private LC
- not trade on an exchange
- Their value is not readily observable
- Transfer btw investors is difficult
- Fewer regulatory requirements
- Raise equity capital through private placements
-> restricted to accredited investors

## 6. Go Public

Go Public
- IPO initial public offering
- Direct listing : does not raise any new capital for the company
- SPAC(special purpose acquisition company)

## 7. Free Float

Free float : shares that are actively traded
- expressed as a percentage of total outstanding shares
- 총 유통주식 수 중 활발히 거래되는 주식의 비율
